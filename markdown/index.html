# Road trip brief

Working plan · updated September 15, 2026

Help two longtime friends choose the next stop on a flexible Denver-to-Las Vegas road trip. Suggest 2–3 practical options, with approximate driving time, effort and weather tradeoffs. Keep answers short and mobile-friendly. Search current sources for weather, trail access, opening hours and availability; link them and distinguish confirmed facts from guesses. Never claim a booking was made. Do not assume the website has changed because we discussed a change in chat.

Dates: Denver airport arrival September 19, 2026 at 13:35 local; Las Vegas departure October 3 at 19:20 local. No Denver sightseeing. Final Vegas night October 2. Tent camping in suitable weather; hostel/motel in rain. Shared room budget roughly $120–140/night. An SUV carries people/gear; sleeping two in it is an emergency fallback, not a highlight.

Interests: mountain towns, short hikes, scenery, beer, tacos/burgers/BBQ, informal American events. Hard hikes, MTB and Telluride via ferrata are optional if both want them. One friend knows Colorado/Moab well; the other is visiting most of the US for the first time and knows the Alps. No orchard-tourism interest. Big B’s near Paonia is simply an optional lunch/cider/siesta stop if passing.

Meals and pubs, optional rafting/guided Jeep outing, Dead Horse Point, Mill Canyon dinosaur sites. MTB choices: Moab Brands easier, Navajo Rocks intermediate, Slickrock technical, Raptor Route shuttle, Ahab expert, Whole Enchilada/Porcupine Rim big shuttle day.
Sep27–28: Preferred southern desert route: Monument Valley → Page. Antelope Canyon guided tour target Sep28, after Page overnight27. Authorized tour operator required. A North Rim detour is optional, not on the direct route, and needs current access checks.
Sep29–Oct1: Zion base. Angels Landing permit-dependent, Narrows condition-dependent; keep other walks and rest time available.
Bryce remains possible with SOUTHERN route: day trip from Zion on Sep30, about2–2.5h each way, or AntelopeSep28 then drive3–3.5h Page→Bryce and overnight near Bryce; morningSep29 at Bryce then2–2.5h toZion. A late Antelope slot makes that rushed. No extra trip nights.

Northern desert route via Capitol Reef/Torrey, Escalante and Bryce remains a comparison alternative, not the current preference. Capitol Reef appeal is cliffs/UT-24/Hickman Bridge, not orchards.

A fork in the road: two ways to Zion

From Moab, both branches end in Zion Canyon before the last run to Las Vegas (2–3 Oct). Choose the scenery and stops that appeal most.

North · scenic roads and varied stops

Torrey / Capitol Reef → Escalante → Bryce

Tentative: September 27–28

Capitol Reef cliffs
Escalante canyon country
Bryce hoodoos

Best for a scenic drive with several different kinds of stops.

WHAT YOU GET
UT-24 cliffs and Highway 12’s changing landscapes
Short rim walks at Bryce; longer hikes if wanted
Capitol Reef: Hickman Bridge or a scenic-drive stop
Escalante adds canyon walks and small-town food stops
WHAT YOU GIVE UP / PLAN AROUND
More winding-road time; two days means choosing highlights
High-elevation nights can be cold
Slot-canyon plans depend on rain, road conditions and access
South · iconic desert landmarks

Monument Valley → Page / Antelope Canyon

Tentative: September 27–28

Monument Valley
Antelope Canyon
Grand Canyon · separate detour

Best if Monument Valley and Antelope are the must-see sights.

WHAT YOU GET
Monument Valley’s sandstone buttes and Western-film scenery
A Navajo-guided Antelope Canyon tour
Horseshoe Bend and Lake Powell near Page
Option to trade other stops for a Grand Canyon detour
WHAT YOU GIVE UP / PLAN AROUND
Antelope needs a timed guided-tour booking
Longer exposed desert stretches and limited shade
Grand Canyon adds substantial driving; it is not on the direct road to Zion
Southern route, with Bryce too

Zion / Springdale ↔ Bryce: about 2–2½ hours each way. A day trip means roughly 4–5 hours driving, plus park time.

Keep the Zion base and visit Bryce on September 30, weather permitting. That uses one Zion day without adding a hotel move.

Or visit Bryce between Page and Zion

September 28: Antelope Canyon, then roughly 3–3½ hours to Bryce if the tour finishes early enough. Stay near Bryce. September 29: morning viewpoints or a hike, then 2–2½ hours to Zion. A later Antelope tour means an extra Page night and less time in Zion.

NPS drive estimate (https://www.nps.gov/zion/frequently-asked-questions-about-other-area-attractions.htm) · Allow more time for stops, park traffic and the Zion tunnel.

Both arrive at Zion Canyon · riverside walks and shuttle-served big-wall trails · then ~2.5 h to Las Vegas.
