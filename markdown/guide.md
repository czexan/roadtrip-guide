# Denver to Las Vegas: full guide

Snapshot September 15, 2026. Contains alternatives, not a promise to visit every place. The trip brief defines the tentative route.

Denver → Las Vegas · 19 Sep – 3 Oct 2026
High Country to Red Rock

Mountain towns, short hikes, big views, good food and beer. Two weeks from Colorado to the desert, with room to change plans.

The route at a glance

Denver airport → Breckenridge/Frisco area → Colorado options → Moab → two desert routes → Zion → Las Vegas.

Two route options from Moab to Zion

Choose one main route between Moab and Zion. The northern route follows Capitol Reef, Escalante and Bryce; the southern route visits Monument Valley and Page. Combining both complete routes adds substantial driving and needs more days. Bryce alone can still be added to the southern route, as shown below.

Southern route, with Bryce too

Zion / Springdale ↔ Bryce: about 2–2½ hours each way. A day trip means roughly 4–5 hours driving, plus park time.

Keep the Zion base and visit Bryce on September 30, weather permitting. That uses one Zion day without adding a hotel move.

Or visit Bryce between Page and Zion

September 28: Antelope Canyon, then roughly 3–3½ hours to Bryce if the tour finishes early enough. Stay near Bryce. September 29: morning viewpoints or a hike, then 2–2½ hours to Zion. A later Antelope tour means an extra Page night and less time in Zion.

NPS drive estimate (https://www.nps.gov/zion/frequently-asked-questions-about-other-area-attractions.htm) · Allow more time for stops, park traffic and the Zion tunnel.

Both arrive at Zion Canyon · riverside walks and shuttle-served big-wall trails · then ~2.5 h to Las Vegas.

## Page camping, football and Las Vegas choices — checked September 15, 2026

- Utah route choice: Monument Valley → Page / Antelope Canyon OR Capitol Reef → Escalante → Bryce. Both end at Zion. Bryce alone can be added to the southern route.
- Page, September 27 night: Lone Rock Beach primitive camping offers beach access and lake views, no numbered sites or reservations. $14/vehicle/night plus park admission. Paid primitive camping, not free dispersed land. Soft sand and changing lake levels affect access; bring drinking water. [NPS camping details](https://www.nps.gov/glca/planyourvisit/lone-rock-beach.htm).
- Moab, Friday September 25 at 7 p.m.: Grand County High School vs Whitehorse, homecoming football. School calendar confirms 608 South 400 East, Moab. Ticket prices and attendance not published. [School calendar](https://gchs.grandschools.org/apps/events/).

Check access and water advisories.

A tight departure-day option with the 19:20 international flight: leave early, aim for airport about16:20 and separately allow for bags and transport. Moab football fits more easily. [Official game listing](https://www.allegiantstadium.com/events/detail/unlv-rebels-vs-cal-2026), [UNLV kickoff announcement](https://unlvrebels.com/news/2026/5/27/football-unlv-to-kick-off-season-on-fox-as-part-of-at-least-11-national-tv-appearances.aspx).
